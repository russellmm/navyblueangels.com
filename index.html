Watch from the crowd line, ride along in a chase plane or orbit the formation. Altitude, airspeed and G update live as each jet flies.

Who flies what

Six jets. One team.

The Diamond flies the close formation work, the two solos fly the opposing passes, and all six join as the Delta. Fat Albert, the Marine Corps C-130J, opens the show.

#1 – #4

The Diamond

Lead, right wing, left wing and slot fly loops, rolls and passes with wingtips about 18 inches apart.

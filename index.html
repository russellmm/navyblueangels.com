04 / BEYOND THE FLIGHT PATH

More than
a flyover.

The Blue Angels’ mission is to showcase the teamwork and professionalism of the United States Navy and Marine Corps through flight demonstrations and community outreach.

This guide focuses on what a spectator sees and how the 2026 manual describes each maneuver. It is an independent educational project, with room to grow into pilot biographies, photography, aircraft stories, and show history.
